# Code of Conduct

TraceLens welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as interactions in which someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different experiences and communication styles.
- Accept feedback gracefully and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at valeriia.radchenko.dev@gmail.com with the subject “TraceLens conduct report”. Include relevant context and links where safe; do not post private details in public issues. Reports will be handled with care, sharing information only as needed to review and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participants based on severity and repeated behavior. Participants may ask for clarification or reconsideration through the same private channel. This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors.
